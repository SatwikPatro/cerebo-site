Getting started

Tap + on the home screen to create a deck, or a category to group several decks together. Open a deck and tap + again to add your first card. Apple Intelligence and privacy

Where does Cerebo's AI run?

On Apple Intelligence: either on your device, or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, depending on the choice in Settings → AI Text Generation. Cerebo has no servers of its own, and the developer never receives your prompts, notes or cards.

What is Private Cloud Compute, and is it private?

Private Cloud Compute is Apple's server for Apple Intelligence. It runs a larger model than the one on your device, so long documents, study guides and practice tests come out better on it. Apple uses each request only to answer it, does not keep it, and does not make it accessible to Apple. It needs iOS 27 and an internet connection.

What do Private Cloud, Hybrid and On Device do?

Every AI request in Cerebo is either heavy work or a quick task, and the setting decides where each kind runs:

RequestPrivate CloudHybridOn Device
Heavy work
Cards from a document, photo, scan, notes or recordingCloudCloudDevice
Study guidesCloudCloudDevice
Practice Test questionsCloudCloudDevice
Revising many cards at once in the chatCloudCloudDevice
Quick tasks
Chat repliesCloudDeviceDevice
One card's answer from GenerateCloudDeviceDevice
Checking written answers in a Practice Test or a chat quizCloudDeviceDevice
Questions about how Cerebo worksCloudDeviceDevice
Working out whether a chat message is a question, a quiz or cards to makeDeviceDeviceDevice
  • Private Cloud is the default. Sorting a chat message stays on your device because it is a one-word answer that doesn't need the larger model, and it would use up your daily limit.
  • Hybrid saves your daily limit for the heavy work. A quick task that is too long for your device's model moves to Private Cloud Compute as it runs.
  • On Device never uses Private Cloud Compute.
  • If Private Cloud Compute can't answer (no connection, an outage, a timeout or the daily limit), your device's model answers instead, when your device has one. Stopping a request, or a request Apple Intelligence declines, never switches to the other model.

Is there a limit on Private Cloud Compute?

Yes, Apple sets a daily limit. Settings → AI Text Generation shows whether you are near it, and when it resets if Apple says. When you reach it, Cerebo carries on with your device's model until the limit resets, and the chat tells you so. Hybrid makes the limit last longer, and a larger iCloud+ plan raises it.

Which devices can use the AI features?

Apple Intelligence needs a device that supports it, turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri, in a supported language and region, on iOS 26 or later. Private Cloud Compute also needs iOS 27 and an internet connection. Cerebo itself runs on iOS 18 and later.

Does Cerebo work without Apple Intelligence?

Yes. Making and editing cards, studying, Match, search, import and export, widgets, iCloud sync and the Apple Watch app work on every supported device. The chat, Practice Test and the Generate button need Apple Intelligence and are hidden without it. Scan to Cards and Record a Lecture then put what you scan or record on a new card, and anything you share to Cerebo can go on a new card or wait until Apple Intelligence is ready.

Does Cerebo work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection except iCloud sync and Private Cloud Compute. Offline, AI requests use your device's own model when it has one. Cerebo makes no network requests of its own.

What does Cerebo collect?

Nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your cards stay on your device and in your own private iCloud account, and chats are kept on the device you had them on. The details are in the privacy policy.

Making cards

How do I make cards from a PDF, notes or a photo?

Tap Ask or make cards to open the chat, then attach a file or photo, scan a page, paste your notes or record a lecture. Cerebo drafts the cards, with tables, equations and graphs where the material has them, and nothing is added until you review the draft and tap Add. A long document can become a category with a deck for each part.

Is there a page limit for PDFs?

Cerebo reads the whole file, then makes cards from a range of pages at a time: up to 120 pages with Private Cloud Compute and up to 30 on the device. Choose the pages in the chat, then carry on with the next range. Pages with a lot of text take longer.

Can I share a web page or a file to Cerebo?

Yes. Choose Make Cards in Cerebo in the share sheet, in Safari or any app that shares text or files, and the chat opens with it the next time you open Cerebo. Without Apple Intelligence, Cerebo asks whether to put it on a new card or keep it for later, and it stays until you use it or choose Discard. Cerebo can't download a video from a link (YouTube, TikTok): save or screen-record the video and share the file, or share YouTube's transcript page from Safari.

Can I ask the chat how to use Cerebo?

Yes. Ask it things like “How do I share notes to Cerebo?” or “What does Hybrid do?”, and it answers from Cerebo's own help pages.

Can I write maths and code on a card?

Yes. In the card editor, expand the editor and use the toolbar to insert a math block (LaTeX), a code block with syntax highlighting, or a graph of one or more functions. You can also write inline maths in text with \(…\), and build tables with ordinary markdown pipes. In the expanded editor, tap the title to switch between the front and the back.

How do cloze deletions work?

Wrap the part you want hidden in double braces: The capital of France is {{c1::Paris}}. Use c1, c2 and so on to reveal blanks separately, and add a hint with a second pair of colons: {{c1::Paris::the city of light}}.

How do I bring my Anki or Quizlet decks across?

Anki: export your deck from Anki as an .apkg file, then in Cerebo tap + on the home screen and choose Import from Anki. Cards, media, cloze deletions, reverse cards and scheduling all come across, and nested Anki decks become Cerebo categories.

Quizlet: use your set's Export on Quizlet's website (in Safari or on a computer), copy the text it gives you, then tap + and choose Import from Quizlet and paste it. Cerebo doesn't download anything from Quizlet.

Cerebo can export back to .apkg at any time, so your cards are never locked in.

Studying

How does the scheduling work?

Cerebo uses the SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm, as Anki does. After you see a card's answer you rate it Again, Hard, Good or Easy, and the time under each button is when the card comes back: easy cards move out to longer intervals, difficult ones return sooner. Each deck introduces 20 new cards a day by default; change it in Settings, or for one deck in Edit Deck. Reviews that are due are never held back.

Why does a card come back a few minutes later?

New cards, and cards you forget, go through two short learning steps, 1 minute and 10 minutes, before moving to days. Cerebo shows you other cards in the meantime. If none are ready, it shows how long until the next one and brings it back when it is due, or you can close the session and come back later. Easy skips the learning steps.

Can I test myself another way?

Yes. A deck's Test button offers Match, where you pair each front with its back against the clock, on every device. With Apple Intelligence it also offers a Practice Test of multiple choice, true or false and written questions, and you can ask the chat to quiz you.

How do I find a card?

Tap the magnifying glass to search categories, decks and the text inside your cards. Tapping a card opens it in its deck, and when the words you searched for are on the back, the result shows where.

Sync and backup

My decks aren't syncing between devices

  1. Check that iCloud sync is turned on in Cerebo's Settings on every device.
  2. Make sure all devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud Drive enabled.
  3. Sync needs a network connection, and can take a few minutes to settle after a large import.
  4. Cerebo's Settings screen shows the current sync status and reports the last error if one occurred.

How do I back up my cards?

Open a deck, tap the ⋯ menu and choose an export format: JSON keeps the most detail, .apkg is best if you want to open the deck in Anki, and CSV is the simplest to read elsewhere. Print or Export PDF gives you every card on paper. Save the file wherever you keep backups.

Do my chats sync?

No. Chats are kept on the device you had them on until you delete them (swipe a chat in the history, or Clear All). Cards and decks made in a chat are part of your library and sync like the rest.

iPad, iPhone Duo, Apple Watch and widgets

Does Cerebo work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. On iPad, and on iPhone Duo unfolded, your library sits in a sidebar beside the deck you have open. Folded halfway on iPhone Duo, Cerebo splits at the hinge: when you study, the card is on one half and the rating buttons on the other, and the chat and the expanded card editor do the same.

Can I use Cerebo on my Apple Watch?

Yes. Install the Cerebo watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone. Your decks sync across from your phone so you can review on your wrist.

What widgets and controls are there?

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show what's due, and a Live Activity follows a study session. The Quick Capture widget and the Scan to Cards and Record a Lecture controls, for Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button, start a scan or a recording in one tap.

Privacy

Cerebo collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Apple Intelligence requests are processed privately on your device or by Apple's Private Cloud Compute. The full details are in the privacy policy.
